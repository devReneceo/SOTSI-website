About Linda Francis

About / About Linda

A life devoted to

Authentic Power.

Linda Francis is a co-founder of the Seat of the Soul Institute. She is deeply dedicated to assisting individuals in aligning their personality and soul in order to create authentic power.

“Your emotions tell you what your soul wants you to know.”

— Linda Francis

A life of healing & partnership

Co-founder of the Seat of the Soul Institute.

Linda is a co-founder of the Seat of the Soul Institute with Gary Zukav, a registered nurse and doctor of chiropractic with more than forty years in the healing professions, a teacher of authentic power who began creating it after reading The Seat of the Soul in 1989, co-author with Gary of The Heart of the Soul: Emotional Awareness, a New York Times bestseller, co-author of The Mind of the Soul: Responsible Choice, Thoughts from the Heart of the Soul, and the Self-Empowerment Journal, and co-designer and co-facilitator of the Institute’s curricula and events with Gary for three decades.

Linda’s soul returned home August 2022. She is still with us.
