The Library

Books by Gary Zukav and Linda Francis

Titles

12 books

Recognition

4 consecutive NYT bestsellers

Where to buy

Amazon

Home·Books

  1. Universal Human

    Universal Human is about the dawning potential of a new era in which authentic power fully emerges at the heart of our evolution. No longer will our species be divided by culture, religion, nationality, ethnic group, or gender, but evolving with the allegiance of all to Life first and everything else second. This is Gary's latest book.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗ Learn more →

  2. The Seat of the Soul

    The Seat of the Soul celebrates the birth of human consciousness beyond the five senses and the intellect, our new understanding of power, and the new requirements of our evolution. A New York Times bestseller for three years.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  3. The Dancing Wu Li Masters

    The Dancing Wu Li Masters is the delightful bible for non-physicists who want to know about quantum mechanics, particle physics, and relativity. This is Gary's first book. Winner of The American Book Award for Science.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  4. The Heart of the Soul

    The Heart of the Soul shows you the importance of your emotions in your spiritual growth and how to use your emotions consciously in your daily life to grow spiritually. Filled with practical exercises.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  5. The Mind of the Soul

    The Mind of the Soul can dramatically change your life by showing you how to take responsibility for the choices you make and free yourself from the illusion that you are a victim of your circumstances.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  6. Spiritual Partnership

    In Spiritual Partnership, bestselling author Gary Zukav reveals a profound new relationship dynamic that enables us to reach our full potential and create authentic power and a joyful life.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  7. Thoughts from the Seat of the Soul

    Thoughts from the Seat of the Soul offers an inspirational excerpt from The Seat of the Soul, one for each day of the year, for your reflection and enrichment. Small enough to take with you everywhere.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  8. Thoughts from the Heart of the Soul

    Thoughts from the Heart of the Soul is a collection of meditations to help you use your emotions consciously to create meaning, purpose, and joy in your life. Small enough to take with you anywhere.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  9. Self-Empowerment Journal

    Self-Empowerment Journal helps you see, in practical, grounded, and relevant ways, that choice equals creation, and the only things preventing you from giving the gifts you were born to give are matters of choice. Your choice.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  10. Soul Stories

    Soul Stories is filled with marvelous stories that show how concepts such as intuition, harmony, cooperation, sharing, and reverence for Life actually express themselves in people's lives. Wonderfully inspiring.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  11. Soul to Soul

    Soul to Soul is a special collection of fifty brief Soul Stories and nearly one hundred answers to Soul Questions. It is profoundly simple, wise, and poetic. An always-fresh source of guidance and inspiration.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗

  12. Soul to Soul Meditations

    Soul to Soul Meditations presents 365 inspirational excerpts, each offering penetrating spiritual and psychological insights for reflection and enrichment. Small enough to fit into your pack or purse.

    Buy Now · Amazon ↗
